# Nexus privacy

Nexus is a local assistant for your phone.

- Country, home, office, and people you save stay on the device.
- That data is excluded from Android backup.
- There is no account and no analytics.
- Place suggestions send the address you type to a map search service so a match can be filled in.
- Contacts are read only after you agree, and only to find a name you asked to call or message.
- Nexus does not read the screens of Uber, Ola, Rapido, or any other app, and it cannot see their fares.
- Opening a cab, map, phone, or chat app hands the address or number to that app. You confirm the fare, call, or message there.
