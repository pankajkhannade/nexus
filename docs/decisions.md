- 2026-11-10: Voice-dictated messages added at Pankaj's request ("parents telling the app to whatsapp or SMS the person about something"). The parent says e.g. "पंकजला व्हॉट्सॲप कर आणि सांग की ..."; Nexus finds the person (fuzzy, with Marathi/Hindi case endings and recogniser misspellings), the app (WhatsApp or SMS) and the message, tidies it with on-device rules (question mark, sentence split, capitals; words kept as said), reads it back, and opens WhatsApp (wa.me link, com.whatsapp or com.whatsapp.w4b) or the SMS app (ACTION_SENDTO smsto: with sms_body) with the text typed in. The parent taps Send there. Reason: WhatsApp has no API for an app to send on the user's behalf, and SEND_SMS is a Play-restricted permission; a prefilled draft also means nothing goes out without the parent's tap. Counted as a local "messages" usage event (counts only, never the text). Gemini Nano polishing was not wired: the app has no on-device LLM flag yet, and the rules are enough for Phase 1.
